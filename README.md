# Paws Field

Paws Field is a small 2D farming game where you play as a cat and take care of your farm. You can explore the map, collect resources, grow crops, interact with things around the farm and look after the animals.

## What you can do

* Explore the farm and move around the map
* Chop trees and collect logs
* Mine rocks and collect stone
* Open and close doors
* Pick up items and keep them in your inventory
* Till soil and plant corn and tomatoes
* Water crops and let them grow over time
* Harvest fully grown crops
* Walk around with chickens and cows
* Talk to characters around the farm
* Save and load your progress
* Watch the day change from morning to night

## Controls

| Key           | Action                                    |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| W / A / S / D | Move                                      |
| Arrow Keys    | Move                                      |
| Left Click    | Use selected tool / interact with objects |
| Right Click   | Cancel selected tool                      |
| E             | Talk / interact                           |
| P             | Save game                                 |
| Esc           | Open game menu                            |

The tools can be selected from the toolbar at the bottom of the screen. Some tools become available as you progress.

## Farming

Start by using the hoe on the ground to make tilled soil. Plant corn or tomato seeds in the tilled soil and water them. Crops take time to grow, so you can spend the day exploring, collecting resources and looking after the farm.

The game also has a day and time system. You can change the speed of time using the speed controls, and the environment changes as the day moves toward night.

## Animals

Chickens and cows move around the farm on their own. They have their own animations and movement speeds, so the animals aren't all moving around in exactly the same way.
